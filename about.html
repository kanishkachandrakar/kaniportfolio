About Kanishka

Computer science graduate student who likes turning ideas into clean, usable software.

Hi! I’m Kanishka Chandrakar — a computer science enthusiast who loves turning ideas into clean, usable software.

I completed my BS in Computer Science at Penn State, and I’m currently pursuing my MS in Computer Science at NYU Courant with a focus on AI & Machine Learning. These days I spend most of my time coding, experimenting with models, and breaking things just enough to learn how to fix them.

Outside of coursework and projects, I enjoy debugging with Bollywood & Punjabi songs, exploring new technologies, and collaborating on ideas that make a real impact.
